# Hi, I’m Nicholas 👋

I’m a consultant and product builder exploring how software can make learning, decision-making, and public systems more useful.

My work sits at the intersection of **education, sustainability, urban and transport planning, data, and technology**. I enjoy turning complex ideas into practical experiences people can explore—not just read about.

## What I’m building

| Project | What it explores |
| --- | --- |
| [**DecisionLab**](https://github.com/sirnicson/decisionlab) | A React and TypeScript client-advisory workspace for practising evidence-based decisions through original environmental cases. |

## How I work

I build through research, rapid prototyping, testing, and steady iteration. Some repositories are finished products; others document the experiments and technical foundations that helped me reach the next one.

Right now, I’m especially interested in:

- interactive learning and serious games;
- decision-support experiences;
- civic, urban, and mobility technology;
- responsible ways to build with AI.
